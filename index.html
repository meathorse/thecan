Q. What the hell is this place?
A. This site achieves a few personal goals

  1. A place to experiment and learn HTML, CSS and more
    2.

    It will never be complete, it'll be lucky to be consistent and generally functional, it's not intended as a serious project

  2. A place to dump my thoughts and ideas
    3.

    Likewise, these may be patchy, probably not well thought out or well-constructed arguments. I'm not looking for an audience else I would post on Social Media, this is purely for my own sanity so I can get my ideas on 'paper' and clear them out of my mind.

  3. A place to write
    4.

    Something that I haven't done in years but I enjoyed writing in my younger years (I'm not that old!)
    Stupid stories, brainstorming ideas or arguing with myself why something is dumb and broken and how I would fix it if I was a dictator! Again, never to be taken seriously and probably too wordy to be considered good but I aim to improve over time.

  4. A poor-man's ode to my favourite tech websites
    5.

    Over the years, we have lost many great websites, I can't ever replace them but I will probably post my own thoughts and items across similar variety of topics. I'm a life-long learner and these sites informed much of my news-feed during my early career and heavily influence my interests. Many of these that are now defunct and most of those still lucky enough to remain are a shadow of their former selves any typically run by corporate entities that pump out souless adverts or press releases maskarading as news and reviews.

    • arstechnica.com
    • anandtech.com
    • techreport.com
    • bit-tech.net
    • hardocp.com
    • tomshardware.com
    • firingsquad.com
    • and many more...
